About Stonecare

One partner for essential supplies and services.

Stonecare Solutions (Private) Limited is a Zimbabwean company incorporated on 3 March 2025. We aim to be a dependable supply and service partner for healthcare providers, mines, industrial sites, commercial businesses and institutions.

Our portfolio brings together healthcare products, workplace protection, rehabilitation support and cleaning solutions—helping clients source products according to their requirements.

  • Responsive communication and professional conduct
  • Fit-for-purpose sourcing based on client requirements
  • Clear quotations and confirmed delivery arrangements
  • Safety-conscious and responsible service
